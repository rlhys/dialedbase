# Kim Logistics - design lock

Sacred logos:
- `public/images/logo-wordmark-chalk.png` (white marks for dark surfaces)
- `public/images/logo-wordmark-ink.png` (near-black marks for light surfaces)

Rules:
- Light KIM + bold LOGISTICS, tagline KEEP IT MOVING
- House outline + four diagonal motion hashes on the baseline
- Chalk on dark. Ink on light. Never `filter: invert` on the logo.
- Logo in header + footer only. Hero uses typographic brand signal (`KEEP IT MOVING`), not the wordmark PNG.
- Palette: black / white high contrast. One restrained CTA accent (`--signal: #ff5c1a`) only.
- Header: solid surfaces only — ink over the hero, solid paper after scroll. No frosted glass / backdrop-blur.
- Craft: premium Coventry operator. Geometric hashes/slashes. Controlled negative space. Full-bleed brand-first hero.
- Type: Space Grotesk (display) + IBM Plex Sans (body). Not Inter / Roboto / Arial.
- Motion: restrained Lenis + GSAP ScrollTrigger FadeIn. Calm hover (color / slight scale). No magnetic chase.
- Forbidden: Dialedbase glass twin, purple gradients, Wix teal clutter, cream/terracotta AI look, keyword-spam copy, invented reviews, frosted sticky headers, magnetic button shake
